Our story

Made with care in Stow-on-the-Wold

The Hive is a warm, rustic café on Digbeth Street serving breakfasts, delicious lunches and fresh coffee. Locals come back for the homemade baked beans and the cakes, and four-legged guests get a treat at the door.
