Our story

Made with care in Chipping Campden

Tucked behind Sheep Street in the 18th-century Old Silk Mill, Campden Coffee & Crystals pairs homemade cakes, breakfasts and milkshakes with a hand-picked collection of crystals. Exposed stone, beams and twinkling fairy lights make it one of Campden’s cosiest corners, and well-behaved dogs are welcome.
