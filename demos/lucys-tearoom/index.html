Our story

Made with care in Stow-on-the-Wold

Lucy's sits in a Grade II listed building on The Square, with exposed beams, flagstone floors and an inglenook fireplace inside, and a sunny courtyard outside. Breakfasts, lunches and traditional afternoon teas are made with home-grown or local ingredients wherever possible, and there are two ensuite B&B rooms upstairs.
